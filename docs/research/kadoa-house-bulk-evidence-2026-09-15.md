# Kadoa and official House bulk evidence

Checked **2026-09-15 (UTC)**. Bounded research for [Verify Kadoa coverage and official House bulk artifact evidence](https://github.com/AviSharma01/signal-workspace/issues/11); evidence only, no source selection or architecture approval. Assess against [approved objectives and constraints](https://github.com/AviSharma01/signal-workspace/issues/2#issuecomment-5677296093): both chambers where defensible, minimum complete 2021–2025 plus 2026, daily discovery and catch-up, deterministic core with no required paid data.

## Result

Kadoa has an accessible free public snapshot with both chambers and original-document links. Its advertised history could span the required years, but this check does **not** establish complete coverage, faithful preservation of raw values, or reliable daily catch-up. One sampled House row loses a source comment. A current generation timestamp is not source freshness or historical availability evidence.

The official **2025FD.ZIP is an index, not a collection of filings**. Its DocID successfully locates a separate original PTR PDF. Its nominal Year=2025 records include FilingDate values in 2026: filtering discovery only by the current year or latest filing date cannot be assumed safe. Neither source's reliability or historical first-publication timestamps are verified.

## Kadoa: claims versus checks

**Documented claims.** The [official README](https://github.com/kadoa-org/congress-trading-monitor/blob/bfd74fa9e2ec3ca45e8c89e046d9bed1c0903ca7/README.md) advertises 2012–present, House/Senate/OGE, static client-loaded JSON, and collection/parsing/normalization through Kadoa. It directs requests for full history with continuous updates to sales; pre-2015 Senate paper PDFs are skipped, and live updates remain in its future-work list. This may be stale documentation; it is not proof that current updates do not occur.

The [live dashboard](https://www.kadoa.com/congress) advertises 68,167 trades from 447 filers, daily updates and free open-source access. The [dataset catalog](https://www.kadoa.com/datasets) similarly claims daily refresh. The [About page](https://www.kadoa.com/congress/about) qualifies original-document/portal links as available where present. These are provider claims, not audited completeness or latency measurements.

**Observed metadata.** Pinned [stats.json](https://github.com/kadoa-org/congress-trading-monitor/blob/bfd74fa9e2ec3ca45e8c89e046d9bed1c0903ca7/public/data/stats.json) reports 68,167 trades, 447 filers, 55,755 filings and 7,208 parsed filings; source counts are House 45,206, Senate 9,190, OGE 13,771. It reports dateRange 2011-06-20–2026-08-31 and generatedAt 2026-09-14T15:02:12.939Z. These values were read, not independently recomputed. The dateRange field has no explicit temporal definition in this file; it is not a coverage guarantee or publication interval. The filing/parsed counts cannot alone measure missing eligible filings because their populations differ or may contain ineligible forms.

**Observed rows and schema.** [House sample](https://github.com/kadoa-org/congress-trading-monitor/blob/bfd74fa9e2ec3ca45e8c89e046d9bed1c0903ca7/public/data/filer/house_adam_smith.json) has 18 rows dated 2025. [Senate sample](https://github.com/kadoa-org/congress-trading-monitor/blob/bfd74fa9e2ec3ca45e8c89e046d9bed1c0903ca7/public/data/filer/senate_ronl_wyden.json) has 303 rows with transaction years 2013, 2014, 2016, 2018–2022, 2024–2025; its filing dates span 2014-07-02–2026-08-08. Missing years within a selected person's file do not establish dataset gaps or inactivity. Neither sample verifies 2021–2026 completeness.

Fields observed in both: `id`, `filing_id`, `filer_id`, `source_id`, `transaction_date`, `notification_date`, `filing_date`, `owner`, `ticker`, `asset_name`, `asset_type`, `transaction_type`, amount low/high/label, `comment`, `is_late`, `days_to_file`, `ingested_at`, `row_index`, `notification_received_over_30d`, `doc_url`, `filing_type`, and return/excess-return fields. These are parsed/normalized/enriched records, not raw source artifacts. Derived metrics were not validated or adopted.

Sample IDs are `house_20030868_t0` / `house_20030868`, and `senate_fe7c39b7-a8ee-4011-b0f6-ab3a320546a0_t0` / the corresponding prefixed filing ID. Filing suffixes match the IDs in their official document URLs; the `_t0` row identifier appears provider-generated and has no verified stable native-row semantics. All 321 sample rows have doc_url; all have null row_index. No raw PDF/HTML content, checksum, source-first-seen timestamp, revision lineage or supersedes field appears in those rows. `ingested_at` is provider metadata without a timezone in sampled strings and cannot establish when the public could first access the filing.

**Provenance check.** The [House PDF linked by Kadoa](https://disclosures-clerk.house.gov/public_disc/ptr-pdfs/2025/20030868.pdf) opens and identifies Adam Smith and filing 20030868. Its first ABBNY row agrees on owner SP, 2025-08-08 transaction/notification dates and $1,001–$15,000 range; PDF transaction code S is normalized to Sale (Full) in JSON. The PDF contains a substantial inherited-IRA/divestment comment for that row, while Kadoa's `comment` is null. Thus even a working original link does not make the JSON lossless. The sampled Senate original URL returned a web-tool Internal Error; its content/provenance correspondence remains unchecked, not disproved.

## Access, cost and retention

Public GitHub content was readable without a Kadoa account or fee; the dashboard calls itself free. The repo's [MIT license](https://github.com/kadoa-org/congress-trading-monitor/blob/bfd74fa9e2ec3ca45e8c89e046d9bed1c0903ca7/LICENSE) grants software/documentation copying with notice retention and no warranty. It is not an explicit dataset-specific perpetual storage contract. Do not infer that a paid continuous feed, raw filing archive, or service API shares identical terms.

[Platform pricing](https://www.kadoa.com/pricing) offers a trial and usage-based tailored quotes, with no verified numeric Congressional-feed price. [Service terms](https://www.kadoa.com/terms), version dated 2023-01-01, describe internal use of extracts, restrictions on commercial exploitation/redistribution and systematic retrieval outside agreement, temporary account access, and no uninterrupted/complete-service warranty. No explicit public-dataset retention duration, update-history retention, post-termination storage permission or dataset-specific override was found in the inspected sources. Applicability differences between the MIT repository and contracted services remain unresolved; this report makes no legal determination. A paid feed cannot become a core dependency under the approved cost constraint without explicit approval; free snapshot access alone does not prove a sustainable $0 daily source.

Bytes **105,770**; SHA-256 **defcdfae19f9b9677ffb600722c5344175ecb1fd0acbdd542a48896a8bb4aaf8**.

Exactly two archive entries: `2025FD.txt` (152,408 uncompressed bytes, tab-separated header) and `2025FD.xml` (795,777 bytes, root FinancialDisclosure). Both archive-entry timestamps are 2026-09-14 09:00:28, with no timezone encoded. No PDF or transaction-detail file is included.

XML has **2,927 records**. Fields: `Prefix`, `Last`, `First`, `Suffix`, `FilingType`, `StateDst`, `Year`, `FilingDate`, `DocID`. All Year values equal 2025. Nonempty FilingDate values span **2025-01-01–2026-09-12**; 15 are empty. This is a nominal/report-year index, not exclusively filings received within calendar 2025. Raw FilingType counts: C 906, X 714, P 515, O 430, D 132, A 115, T 53, W 47, E 5, G 5, H 3, B 2. No code legend is embedded; this report does not guess the non-P meanings or label A as an amendment without verification.

Representative P record: Hon. Robert B. Aderholt, AL04, Year 2025, FilingDate 9/10/2025, DocID 20032062. [Separate official PDF](https://disclosures-clerk.house.gov/public_disc/ptr-pdfs/2025/20032062.pdf) is a one-page PTR carrying filing ID 20032062; its GSK sale is dated 07/28/2025, notification 08/11/2025, signature 09/10/2025. This verifies one DocID/year PTR path and P-to-PTR correspondence. The index contains no URL field; non-P path conventions and complete type meanings remain unchecked. It supports finding filing artifacts, not direct transaction ingestion without retrieving/parsing them. Signature, filing date and HTTP modification date do not establish original public availability.

The [official search page](https://disclosures-clerk.house.gov/FinancialDisclosure/ViewSearch) displays statutory use restrictions including commercial use exceptions and prohibited solicitation/credit-rating uses. Public access is not unrestricted-use authorization. One archive and one locator do not establish multi-year completeness, preservation policy or reliable service availability.

## Daily discovery and catch-up limits

| Source | What is supported | What remains unverified |
|---|---|---|
| Kadoa public snapshot | Downloadable static files and original URLs; recent provider generation metadata; advertised daily refresh. | Dataset-specific incremental API/cursor, pagination limits, update watermark semantics, retention/deletion policy, stable row IDs through corrections, amendment linkage, guaranteed recovery after arbitrary downtime, and measured refresh lag against official new filings. README/live-page cadence claims are not reconciled by one observation. |
| House annual index | One complete downloaded archive has no pagination; DocID supports one original PTR retrieval. HTTP validators identify the current representation. | Refresh SLA, change log/cursor, amendment linkage, past-version retention, all filing-type paths, recovery from missed transient records, multi-year update behavior and completeness. Current artifact includes next-year filing dates, so a current-year-only/date-watermark catch-up assumption is unsupported. |

**Inference, not implementation approval:** comparing retained snapshots by source identifiers could detect records still present after downtime, and rereading relevant past-year House indexes may be necessary. Neither alone proves that removed/replaced records or amendments are recovered. No repeated-day observation, deliberate outage simulation, official-to-Kadoa reconciliation or amendment pair was checked. Reliable daily catch-up therefore remains **unverified for both**.

**Access limits:** initial local curl DNS resolution failed in the sandbox; approved retry succeeded. One GitHub sample read had an environment/network failure; approved retry succeeded. Direct web rendering of the House portal returned mostly footer content although search indexing exposed its annual-download section; the ZIP itself was successfully fetched. Senate sample PDF/HTML retrieval through web returned Internal Error. These are check-specific failures, not findings of general source unavailability.

## Handoff

The advertised Kadoa span is potentially long enough, but no source in this check is certified to meet the complete 2021–2026 requirement. Free public access and the official House index provide concrete candidate evidence; reliable chamber/year coverage, raw fidelity, source freshness, amendment handling, ongoing rights/cost and catch-up semantics remain explicit tradeoffs for [Choose historical and ongoing disclosure source strategy](https://github.com/AviSharma01/signal-workspace/issues/4). No renewed approval of shorter history, paid data, or historical availability assumptions is implied.
