# Historical security identity for the free Signal V2 composite

Research observation date: **2026-10-09**. Bounded research and decision support only. No code changes, provider integration, issue/readiness changes, Git operations, account creation, identity API experiment or universe download. This report recommends further investigation; it approves neither architecture implementation nor a provider.

**An evidence-backed free identity layer is realistically plausible for a bounded, measured population. A complete, retainable 2021–2026 U.S.-listed historical security master is not established.** The strongest components are original EDGAR filings, OpenFIGI enrichment, Nasdaq's expressly free issue/status data, and NYSE's public Market Event Feed (MEF). None alone supplies historical ticker validity, stable security identity, primary-listing history, publication vintages and reproducible retained evidence.

Success criteria were primary-source evidence, all fourteen requested properties classified, the specified collision/terminal cases examined, a conditional composite recommendation and one smallest next experiment. Work proceeded through contract inspection, official documentation/terms, dated case evidence and citation review. No workload or complete historical inventory was measured.

### SEC/EDGAR originals: strongest historical evidence route

**D:** EDGAR permits free downloads and declared automated access subject to a maximum of ten requests/second. Indexes extend to 1994Q3. CIKs are nonrecycled identifiers for **filers**, not each security or listing. Current company/ticker association files lack completeness/accuracy guarantees. Post-acceptance corrections and removals can change archive/index contents. **I:** archive span supports researching 2021–2026, but does not establish complete security-event discovery or untouched original versions. [SEC access documentation](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-search-assistance/accessing-edgar-data).

**D:** Submissions metadata has current ticker/exchange information and accession history; additional files carry older filings. This is a discovery route, not dated ticker validity. Companyfacts covers standard-taxonomy, entire-entity facts; security-specific cover contexts require original filing examination. [SEC API documentation](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-application-programming-interfaces).

**D:** Form 10-K covers identify registered class title, trading symbol and exchange. Except explicitly dated information, the instructions call for the latest practicable information. A fiscal-year duration context therefore cannot be used as a year-long ticker-validity interval. Cover iXBRL phased in through 2021 and does not apply uniformly to all fund/non-operating entities. Parse original class contexts and text, retaining their actual meanings. [Form 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/files/form10-k.pdf), [cover-tagging rule](https://www.sec.gov/files/rules/final/2019/33-10618.pdf).

**D:** Applicable registered funds have persistent SEC series (S) and class (C) IDs under specified registration forms. A trust CIK can contain many funds/classes. SEC requires updates for ticker changes and inactive/merged classes and describes correction/refiling of erroneous IDs. **I:** these IDs are valuable for the two ETF stress cases, but their exact historical S/C assignments were not verified here; current series/class files cannot be backdated. [Series/class FAQ](https://www.sec.gov/info/edgar/ednews/seriesclassfaq063006.htm), [identifier rule](https://www.sec.gov/rules/final/33-8590fr.pdf).

**Can resolve:** original cover pages establish particular class/ticker/exchange assertions; explicit 8-Ks, prospectus supplements, issuer exhibits and exchange-filed Form 25s can establish rename, transfer, cancellation, suspension and removal boundaries. **Cannot resolve automatically:** every intervening day, every corporate successor, missing events, stale disclosure symbols or all securities under one filer. A security may be replaced while its ticker remains unchanged.

**Rights clarification, 2026-10-15:** SEC explicitly permits access/reuse of EDGAR public filing content, and its dissemination policy permits copying/distribution and requests appropriate attribution. This corrects the earlier access-only assessment; permission need not depend on treating issuer-authored material as a government work. Removed-version availability and complete retained-version coverage remain untested, and the permission does not extend to non-SEC issuer/exchange sites or proprietary crosswalks. [SEC reuse FAQ](https://www.sec.gov/about/webmaster-frequently-asked-questions), [dissemination policy](https://www.sec.gov/about/privacy-information#website-dissemination).

### OpenFIGI: stable IDs with favorable reuse rights

**D:** FIGIs are permanent and nonrecycled. Equity identity distinguishes share class, country/market composite and trading venue; ticker renames preserve identity, and delisted assignments remain. **I:** a U.S. composite or trading-venue FIGI is not a dated primary-listing assertion. A stock can trade on multiple venues; primary listing must come from separate evidence. [Overview](https://www.openfigi.com/about/overview), [allocation rules](https://www.openfigi.com/about/allocation-rules), [allocation reference](https://www.openfigi.com/docs/figi-allocation-rules.pdf).

**D:** v3 mapping supports proprietary-ID inputs, MIC/exchange filters and `includeUnlistedEquities=true`. Outputs provide FIGI hierarchy and descriptive attributes, but no equity historical as-of parameter, symbol-validity bounds or event/publication/revision times. Date filters concern expiration/maturity, not historical equity lookup. [Official schema](https://api.openfigi.com/schema). Free access exists with and without a key. Documentation conflicts on unauthenticated batch limits (ten versus five); a two-job request fits both. [API documentation](https://www.openfigi.com/api/documentation).

**D:** FIGI strings are dedicated to the public domain, with reproduction/derivative/redistribution rights preserved against subsequent term changes. The FAQ explicitly permits database storage/reuse of FIGI symbology and describes associated metadata as open under MIT. **I:** retaining supplied open mapping responses, observed versions and derived relationships is defensible; this does not license proprietary input IDs. OpenFIGI cannot return a free CUSIP/ISIN crosswalk because of third-party restrictions. No metadata correction chronology is documented. [Terms](https://www.openfigi.com/docs/terms-of-service), [storage/metadata FAQ](https://www.openfigi.com/about/faq).

**Can resolve:** independently identified classes into stable-ID candidates, potentially including retired instruments. **Cannot resolve:** who owned literal FB or META at a historical date, when an alias/listing changed, or what a mapping provider knew then. A mapping retrieved in 2026 remains a 2026 observation. It can enrich a retrospective catalog without authorizing identity-dependent eligibility at a 2021 boundary.

### Nasdaq: distinguish free data from historical alert pages

**D:** Symbol Lookup explicitly describes current-trading-day information. The named Nasdaq Security Status Updates, Ex-Date, When Distributed/When Issued and Nasdaq Listed Events Data have an express unrestricted-use exemption. Directory fields describe symbols, class/issue names, status and exchange; file creation time means generation, not security-valid-from. [Free subset notice](https://www.nasdaqtrader.com/trader.aspx?id=symbollookup), [directory definitions](https://www.nasdaqtrader.com/Trader.aspx?id=SymbolDirDefs).

**D, bounded UI observation:** the free [Status Updates](https://www.nasdaqtrader.com/Trader.aspx?id=nasdaq-security-status-updates) page's date inputs have a 2024-01-01 minimum. Visible rows supply effective date, symbol, issue name and status/event, without permanent issue IDs or publication times. This limits the inspected UI; it does not prove no older records exist elsewhere. The [2026 rule filing](https://www.sec.gov/files/rules/sro/nasdaq/2026/34-106034.pdf) describes freely usable FTP issue/status data and distribution timing, without establishing a 2021 archive. [Daily List](https://www.nasdaqtrader.com/Trader.aspx?id=DailyListPD) history is a separate paid route.

**D:** separate public alerts survive from 2021/2022 and can identify both sides of transitions. FB→META has a specific class, old/new symbols, effective date and unchanged CUSIP. XXII's 2021 transfer has explicit exchange boundaries. A SOAC→TMC alert explicitly updates its proposed effective date from September 8 to September 10, illustrating revisions to future plans. The current page does not supply the original earlier bytes or exact correction-publication instant. [Rename](https://www.nasdaqtrader.com/TraderNews.aspx?id=ECA2022-125), [transfer](https://www.nasdaqtrader.com/TraderNews.aspx?id=dtn2021-31), [date correction](https://www.nasdaqtrader.com/TraderNews.aspx?id=dtn2021-34).

**Rights:** P for the expressly unrestricted subset; **I:** that grant supports local raw/version/derived preservation of that subset. U for treating all historic alerts as a reusable identity database. General terms allow one permanent unaltered personal copy with notices, but broader storage/reuse and CUSIP database extraction are constrained. Do not silently extend the subset grant to every alert, other-listed directory field, Nasdaq.com API or CUSIP. [General terms/CUSIP usage](https://www.nasdaqtrader.com/Trader.aspx?id=CopyDisclaimMain).

**Can resolve:** selected dated renames/transfers and issue statuses, supplemented with security evidence. **Cannot resolve alone:** reused tickers from symbol-only rows, complete pre-2024 history or historical primary-listing intervals from today's directory. Prefer an SEC-filed issuer exhibit when it establishes the same relationship with a better retention route.

### NYSE public MEF: strong grant, uncertain backfill

**D:** NYSE's rule filing covers listing, suspension, delisting, name/symbol changes and issuer lifecycle information, with public exchange-received information accessible without charge for any party/purpose, including redistribution. **I:** local raw/version/derived retention is supported for that exact public subset; the grant does not buy the commercial MEF service. [Public-use rule filing, pp. 2–3](https://www.sec.gov/files/rules/sro/nyse/2024/34-101516.pdf), [commercial MEF](https://www.nyse.com/market-data/corporate-actions/market-event-feed).

**D, bounded read:** the [public directory](https://ftp.nyse.com/MEF_Public/) exposed one [JSON file](https://ftp.nyse.com/MEF_Public/mef_public.json). A MID voluntary-suspension record carried event UUID, symbol, publication October 7, 2026 with Eastern offset, effective October 12 before-open and cancellation status. The UUID identifies an event, not a permanent security. No historical index was exposed. [Corporate Actions UI](https://www.nyse.com/trade/corporate-actions) date controls do not by themselves establish free archival rows.

The MID example above was an earlier browser observation, not a row in the later retained eight-record file. The [bounded public-MEF check](nyse-public-mef-screen-2026-10-09.md) and its receipt identify the exact retained snapshot and narrow durable-retention claims. No missing MID bytes are implied to be present.

**Can resolve:** effective lifecycle events with source publication semantics when matching security evidence exists. **Cannot yet resolve:** 2022 TWTR via the free current file, old ticker owners, class identity, an entire 2021–2026 event chain or old revised versions. Form 25 on EDGAR is a distinct route to NYSE-authored terminal evidence.

## Recommended composite architecture — conditional proposal

Use an **application-owned identity ledger of evidence-backed assertions**, with original EDGAR material as the historical spine, free exchange events for dated transitions and OpenFIGI as optional stable-ID enrichment. Issuer sites are supporting sources where rights and historical availability are established. No source has been selected or approved.

The logical record is:

`security identity + class/type + primary listing/exchange + source ticker + supported validity bounds + optional stable identifiers + evidence/provenance + publication/observation timing + resolution state`

This calls for logical separation, not new table/module proliferation:

1. Separate issuer/filer, security/share class, listing segment and ticker assignment. CIK points to the filer; SEC fund class IDs and FIGI hierarchy are separately typed identifiers. Never use a ticker as the permanent key.
2. Preserve raw source values/artifact versions where rights allow, request/retrieval observation, source document/accession/event ID, content hash and parser/resolution version. Every derived interval endpoint references the evidence establishing it. A source hash establishes observed bytes, not amendment status or historic publication.
3. Keep economic effectiveness, supported public availability and actual local observation separate. Preserve dates, precision, timezone and before-open semantics. Planned events remain planned until supported; cancellation/correction assertions retain their original predecessors. Later knowledge enters a separately versioned retrospective interpretation.
4. Store unknown/open bounds and observation-only assertions honestly. A cover-page observation is not `valid_from`; first retrieved is not first valid; two annual observations do not prove continuity. Derive only the interval segment justified by eligible change/continuity evidence and declared discovery coverage. Do not infer “no event” from absence in an incomplete feed.
5. Resolve for an explicitly stated historical reference date and As-Of perspective. Transaction-era and disclosure-era references may differ; do not silently reinterpret a stale ticker. Missing boundaries, conflicting class evidence or incompatible listing claims produce unresolved/ambiguous states and exact reasons. Confidence records the evidentiary basis, not an LLM probability substituting for proof.
6. Keep provider-specific price symbols downstream of resolved identity/listing. Price similarity is never identity evidence. A verified rename can maintain security identity; reuse creates a separate security. Listing continuity does not approve return treatment across a transfer/terminal event under §6.

**I, feasibility:** target issuer/class discovery from actual disclosure references rather than an initial market-wide master. Backfill originals and dated transitions, checking pre-2021 evidence when needed to establish the starting identity. Subsequently observe free exchange changes and new filings, with bounded catch-up and version comparisons. Request counts, manual-review burden, gaps, elapsed time, memory/storage and offline replay remain E; none was measured. A local forward archive cannot retroactively establish 2021 knowledge vintages.

## Remaining hard UNKNOWNs and decision

- Complete free change discovery for 2021–2026, especially Nasdaq's pre-2024 free status/issue archive and NYSE's historical public MEF. Accessible examples are not a full inventory or survivorship-free coverage.
- Complete retained-version coverage for the exact SEC evidence, and applicable rights for non-SEC issuer material and historic Nasdaq alerts. SEC public-filing reuse permission does not recover missing versions or remove other sources' restrictions; Cboe needs an applicable exception or permission for ledger ingestion.
- Security continuity across replacements, reorganizations, stale tickers and sparse observations; exact fund class IDs; primary listing versus trading venues. A current stable ID alone settles none of these dates.
- Historical public availability of particular versions, correction publication and withdrawn/overwritten evidence. #31 requires preserving versions actually observed, not reconstructing every version ever published, but missing eligible historical identity still cannot be repaired with later facts.
- Actual FIGI retrieval/multiplicity for selected retired classes and the bounded extraction/update/review workload needed to sustain coverage for a solo project.

**Decision:** continue a small composite identity experiment. The required rename, reuse, class and terminal relationships have defensible primary evidence, so free identity is more credible than a literal-ticker join. Universal complete historical coverage and licensed reproducible operation remain unproven. No full historical-identity gate is passed, and no #31 production-readiness state changes.
