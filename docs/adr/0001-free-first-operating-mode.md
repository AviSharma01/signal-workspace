# Free-first operating mode

Status: Accepted on 2026-10-15 by explicit project-owner direction in the
Free-First Productization audit request. This records an operating-mode and
identity-ownership decision; it does not approve an implementation schema,
provider, source license, or operational readiness.

Signal will operate free-only for now and prioritize a useful disclosure-first
workspace. No paid market-data provider, purchase, subscription, or paid-data
trial is authorized. Earlier research recommendations to consider paid data
remain decision support; this decision selects the free-only route. No approved
methodology or readiness requirement is superseded.

The product scope remains official disclosure evidence and provenance,
historically supported identities and use-specific Event eligibility,
disclosure-only coverage and missingness, market-independent Watch Event
lifecycle, evidence/capability inspection, bounded model-independent
investigations, application-validated Findings, and workspace navigation, where
each function's own prerequisites are satisfied. Implementation and synthetic
tests do not establish operational availability. House/Senate source readiness
and historical coverage must retain their actual limitations; this decision
does not certify either chamber or approve deferred non-market anomaly criteria.

Production market-data readiness under [#31](https://github.com/AviSharma01/signal-workspace/issues/31)
remains unavailable. Closing that issue completed the provider-neutral harness,
not provider certification. Market-dependent Analysis, benchmark-adjusted
outcomes, market checks/anomalies, and market-triggered investigations remain
gated. The [approved V2 specification](../specs/signal-v2.md), including its
population, SPY benchmark, total-return methodology, horizons, uncertainty,
missingness, point-in-time boundaries, and unchanged market-data contract,
continues to govern. Historical Raw, Expected, and Excess Consensus remain
deferred. Free mode does not authorize adjusted-price substitution, a
data-available primary population, or weaker evidence requirements.

Historical security identity will be application-owned and evidence-backed,
independent of an eventual price provider. Keep issuer/filer, security/share
class, listing, and dated ticker relationships distinct, with cited assertions,
supported effective bounds, public availability, actual system observation,
version history, and explicit ambiguity. Current tickers, CIKs, or optional
OpenFIGI enrichment cannot alone establish historical continuity. Exact-source
retention remains subject to applicable rights and the governing evidence
contract; this decision grants no retention exception.

The reported validation of `exchange_calendars==4.13.2` makes it a future
integration candidate only. Integration still requires reproducible retained
expectations/output, versioning, and authoritative extraordinary-closure checks;
it does not certify provider bars or activate #31. kdb+/q remains a deferred,
optional time-series capability, requiring a separately approved measured
boundary and independent numerical parity before adoption.

Preserve the existing provider-neutral adapter, retained-provenance, normalized
semantics, conformance, and capability boundaries. A future paid component can
fit those boundaries after a separate explicit project decision, rights review,
independent real-provider validation, and approval under the unchanged contract.
No paid-provider implementation or speculative abstraction is needed now.
